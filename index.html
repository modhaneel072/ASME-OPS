On the bench this year

DBF

Design Build Fly

An unmanned electric radio-controlled aircraft, designed, fabricated, and flown against a mission profile the competition sets.

Proposal Managed by Tyler Tatman

ROV

MATE ROV

A remotely operated underwater vehicle. First year of the program, building the foundation a competition entry will stand on.

Foundation Managed by Corbin Dexter

MED

Medical Device

A medical product taken the whole way through design, prototyping, and fabrication by the team that scoped it.

Prototyping Managed by Ryan Maire

SDC

Student Design Competition

ASME's own competition. Teams design, manufacture, and test an entry against this year's rules, then iterate on what the testing shows.

Iterating Managed by Colin Carroll

IAM3D

Innovative Additive Manufacturing 3D Challenge

ASME's additive manufacturing design challenge. Teams take an entry from CAD to a printed, tested part, and make it competition-ready.

Competition prep Managed by Neel Modha

Every project, past and present

How you get in

  1. 1

    Come to a general meeting

    General body meetings are posted on Engage. Turn up and you are in the room.

  2. 2

    Pick a team

    Talk to the manager whose project you want. DBF, MATE ROV, Medical, SDC, and IAM3D take new members through the year, not only in the fall.

  3. 3

    Get into G440

    Safety and access training clears you for the shop. After that the tools, the printers, and the bench are yours to work on.
