## Roles

- **Chapter Administrator** (`chapter_admin`): All chapter settings, users, data, reporting and audit.
- **Executive Officer** (`executive_officer`): Broad operational access; limited security configuration.
- **Project Lead** (`project_lead`): Manages own projects, their teams, work, assets and reports.
- **Team Lead** (`team_lead`): Creates, assigns and manages work for own team.
- **Full Member** (`full_member`): Executes work, creates permitted work, uses messages and procedures.
- **Shop Operator** (`shop_operator`): Views and completes assigned work; reports issues.
- **Requester** (`requester`): Submits and tracks requests.
- **Inventory Manager** (`inventory_manager`): Parts, transactions, cycle counts and purchase receiving.
- **Safety Officer** (`safety_officer`): Procedures, inspections, corrective actions and safety reporting.
- **Treasurer** (`treasurer`): Purchase approvals, budgets and cost reports.
- **Faculty Advisor** (`faculty_advisor`): Reads everything plus approvals configured by the chapter.
- **Sponsor / Guest** (`sponsor_guest`): Explicitly shared read-only dashboards or reports.
